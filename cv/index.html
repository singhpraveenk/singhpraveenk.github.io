Education

Experience

Postdoctoral Research Scientist · Laboratoire de Météorologie Dynamique (LMD) – IPSL/CNRS, École Polytechnique, Palaiseau, France · April 2026–present
Interactions between atmospheric pollutants and meteorology/climate over South and Southeast Asia.
Senior Program Associate – Air Quality · WRI India, New Delhi · May 2024–March 2026
Worked with the Gorakhpur city government on sector-wise strategies to reduce air pollution.
Senior Research Fellow · Indian Institute of Technology Roorkee · January 2021–April 2024
Space-based monitoring of air pollution from biomass burning over the Indo-Gangetic Plain; regional climate and general circulation modelling on the PARAM Ganga supercomputer.
SSA – Cryosphere and Atmosphere · International Centre for Integrated Mountain Development (ICIMOD), Kathmandu, Nepal · January–December 2020
Operation of SMART and AERONET stations in Nepal; analysis of observational, satellite and gridded atmosphere and cryosphere data; radiative transfer and glacier energy and mass balance modelling.
Research Fellow · ICIMOD, Kathmandu, Nepal · April 2018–December 2019
Set-up and operation of SMART and AERONET stations in Nepal; analysis of station, satellite and gridded datasets.
Junior Research Fellow · Indian Institute of Technology Roorkee · January 2017–March 2018
Glacier-related hazards and atmospheric remote sensing in the Indian Himalaya.

Summer Intern · Physical Research Laboratory, Ahmedabad, India · May–July 2015

Telecom Engineer · Agate Infosoft Pvt. Ltd., Noida, India · January–December 2012
